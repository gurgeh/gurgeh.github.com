Hugin privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Hugin is a set of tools David Fendrich has written for his own use. It is not offered to anyone else. The only Google account it signs in to is his.

Access

Through the Google Photos Library API, Hugin uploads pictures from a trail camera to an album it creates and writes captions for them. It can read only the photos and albums it has uploaded itself. The rest of the photo library is out of its reach.

Storage

Photos, access tokens and other data stay on David's computer and in his Google account. Nothing is passed on to anyone else or used for advertising.

Hugin's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
